# Marketing Strategy

## Positioning

**Tagline:** *Your AI business agent on WhatsApp — in Arabic and English*

**Target:** SMEs in Dubai and UAE that rely on WhatsApp for customer communication but lack 24/7 coverage.

**Differentiators:**
- Built specifically for UAE (Arabic RTL, Dubai timezone, AED pricing)
- Industry templates (real estate, clinics, restaurants)
- All-in-one: AI + CRM + bookings + analytics
- No-code knowledge base training (upload PDFs, go live)

## Target Segments (Priority Order)

1. **Real estate agencies** — high lead value, appointment-heavy
2. **Clinics & salons** — booking automation, reminders
3. **Restaurants** — reservations, menu inquiries
4. **E-commerce** — order tracking, product questions
5. **Travel & car rental** — multilingual support critical

## Launch Plan (90 Days)

### Month 1: Foundation
- Launch landing page + pricing (EN/AR)
- 10 beta customers (free Professional for 3 months)
- Case study: real estate agency (leads +40%, response time -80%)
- Submit to Product Hunt, UAE startup directories

### Month 2: Paid Acquisition
- Google + Meta campaigns (AED 15,000 budget)
- Webinar: "Automate WhatsApp for your Dubai business"
- Partner with 3 business setup firms for referrals

### Month 3: Scale
- Expand to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah via localized ads
- Launch referral program (1 month free per referral)
- Enterprise outreach for multi-branch clinics/restaurants
